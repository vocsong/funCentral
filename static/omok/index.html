    How to Play

    Omok (오목), also called Gomoku, is a game of five in a row. Two players take turns placing black and white stones on the intersections of a 15 × 15 board.

    Make a line of five

    The first unbroken line of five or more stones wins, horizontally, vertically or diagonally. A full board without a winner is a draw. This is freestyle Omok: overlines count, and there are no forbidden double-three or double-four moves.

    Your move

    1. Black moves first. Colours are assigned randomly for the first round and swap every rematch.
    2. Tap or click an empty intersection to preview your stone. You can change your selection freely.
    3. Choose Place to commit. Stones stay where they are placed. The last move has a small centre dot.

    On a small screen, Enlarge board gives you more space. Scroll within the board to reach every point. With a keyboard, use the arrows, Home and End to explore; Enter or Space selects. Tab to Place and press Enter to commit. Escape clears your preview.

    Friends, practice & rematches

    Share your room code or invite link. As host, you can add a practice bot instead. It takes immediate wins, blocks direct threats and builds open lines. Bot games are unrated. Anyone with the invite can watch.

    Leaving mid-round keeps your seat so you can reconnect. Resign round concedes to your opponent; after a result, either player can choose Play Again to return to the lobby.

    Separate Omok ratings

    Human matches use two-player Elo, starting at 1000. K is 16 for your first 30 rated games, then 8. A draw scores 0.5 each. After three consecutive losses to the same opponent, further losses in that streak halve both players’ K again. A win, draw or different opponent breaks the streak. Practice does not affect it.
