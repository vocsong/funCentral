Game log

    How to Play

    Two players take turns dropping a disc into one of seven columns. The disc falls to the lowest empty slot in that column.

    Objective

    Be the first to line up four of your discs in a row — horizontally, vertically or diagonally. If the board fills up with no winner, the game is a draw.

    Turns

    1. Choose your disc colour in the lobby: the host gets Red, Rose or Violet; the second player gets Yellow, Cyan or Mint. The two palettes stay distinct. The first game's opener is chosen randomly, then players alternate opening each rematch. A short border shuffle reveals the opener before anyone can drop a disc.
    2. Click or tap the column you want, and your disc drops into it.
    3. You can't play into a column that is already full.

    Winning & rating

    A win is rated as a standard two-player Elo match; a full-board draw is a 0.5 result for both. The K-factor is 16 for your first 30 rated games, then 8, for gentler rating gains and losses. Games against the practice bot are not rated. Rounds must finish before returning to the lobby. Leaving mid-round keeps your seat and the current game.

    Rematch protection: after three consecutive rated losses to the same opponent, further losses in that streak halve both players' rating changes again, before rounding. A rated win, draw, or game against someone else breaks the streak. Practice games do not affect it.

    Playing solo

    As the host, tap 🤖 Add Bot in the lobby to seat a practice opponent, then start the game. The practice bot looks ahead two plies: it takes immediate wins, blocks obvious threats, and prefers the centre columns. Longer traps can catch it out.
