    Field manual

    How to Play

    Work together to defeat all 12 royals — 4 Jacks, then 4 Queens, then 4 Kings. If anyone can't survive a hit, everyone loses.

    JJack⚔ 10 · ❤ 20
    QQueen⚔ 15 · ❤ 30
    KKing⚔ 20 · ❤ 40

    Your turn

    1. Attack with a card or combo — or yield (not if every other player yielded on their last turn, and never in solo).
    2. Your cards' suit powers trigger.
    3. Deal damage equal to the cards' value. If the enemy falls, you go again against the next royal.
    4. Otherwise it strikes back: discard cards worth at least its attack (minus shields).

    Suit powers

    • ♥
      Heal — shuffle the discard pile and put that many cards under the tavern deck.
    • ♦
      Draw — players draw that many cards in turn, starting with you (no one exceeds their hand size).
    • ♣
      Double — the attack deals double damage.
    • ♠
      Shield — reduce this enemy's attack by that much for the rest of the fight.

    Each enemy is immune to its own suit's power (the damage still counts).

    Combos

    • Animal Companion (A) is worth 1 and pairs with any one other card. Both suits' powers apply.
    • Sets: 2–4 cards of the same rank totalling 10 or less (5+5, 3+3+3, 2+2+2+2).

    Defeating royals

    Hit an enemy for exactly its remaining health and it joins your side — it goes on top of the tavern deck (J=10, Q=15, K=20). Overkill sends it to the discard pile.

    The Jester 🃏

    Play it alone: it cancels the enemy's immunity (spades already played start shielding) and you choose who goes next. No damage is dealt or taken.
