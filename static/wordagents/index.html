      How to Play

      Two teams race to find their secret agents hidden among 25 words. The starting team has nine agents, the other eight. There are also seven civilians and one assassin.

      Teams and roles

      Each team has one spymaster and at least one operative. Only spymasters see the key: which words belong to which team. Four to twelve players can join. Teams and roles are assigned randomly every round.

      Your turn

      1. The spymaster gives one word and a number, e.g. TIDE · 3, linking that many of their team’s agents.
      2. Operatives discuss, mark cards to share ideas, then reveal one card at a time. They may make the number plus one guesses, and must make at least one before ending the turn.
      3. Your agent: keep going. A civilian or the other team’s agent: your turn ends. The assassin: your team loses immediately.

      Fair clues

      Clues are about meaning, not spelling, letter count or board position. A clue can’t be, or contain, a word still on the board (the game blocks these). Agree on borderline clues together.

      Playing remotely

      Coordinates (A1–E5) on every card make it easy to talk through options in chat or on a call. Spymasters should use their own device; roles stay locked until the round ends, including after a reconnect. Word Agents is casual and unrated.
