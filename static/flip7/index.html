    KNOW WHEN TO STOP

    How to play Flip 7

    On your turn, Hit to draw or Stay to bank your round score. Draw a number you already have and you bust: zero points this round.

    Seven is the magic number

    Collect seven different number cards to end the round immediately, adding 15 bonus points. Otherwise, the round ends when everyone has stayed, frozen or busted.

    A few twists

    • Freeze: choose an active player to bank their score and end their round.
    • Flip Three: choose an active player to draw three cards. Resolve Action Cards as instructed.
    • Second Chance: protects against one duplicate number. An extra Second Chance must be passed to another eligible active player.
    • Score Modifiers and ×2: double the sum of your number cards, then add bonus cards. The seven-card bonus is not doubled.

    The higher the number, the more copies in the deck. The first round to take anyone to 200 ends the game; highest total wins. If the lead is tied, play another round to break the tie.
